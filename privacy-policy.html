03 How we collect information

  • Meta Lead Ads / Instant Forms: When you tap "Submit" on a Facebook or Instagram lead ad, the details you enter (often pre-filled by Facebook/Instagram, plus any answers you type) are shared with me through Meta's platform.
  • Messenger conversations: When you message my Facebook Page or reply to an automated greeting, I (or an automated reply, where used) may collect the details of that conversation, including anything you choose to share.
  • The free assessment and website forms: When you take the assessment or fill out a form on insurancebyengrzeth.com, your answers are sent to me and used to generate your personalized Coverage Snapshot by email.
  • Direct communication: Information you provide through email, WhatsApp, phone calls, or in-person or online meetings — including a booked Clarity Call.

04 Why we collect this information

  • To respond to your inquiry and identify you correctly across our conversation.
  • To understand your general insurance or HMO needs and financial goals, so anything I share is actually relevant to you.
  • To prepare and send your personalized Coverage Snapshot, quotation, or assessment result.
  • To follow up, schedule a Clarity Call, or answer your questions.
  • To keep recordkeeping practices reasonably expected of a licensed financial/insurance advisor.

My legal basis for processing this information is your consent — given when you voluntarily submit a lead form, send a Messenger message, or complete the assessment — and, where applicable, my legitimate interest in responding to inquiries directed at my business.

05 How we use your information

  • Reply to you personally — by Messenger, email, SMS, or phone/WhatsApp — about your inquiry.
  • Prepare general insurance or HMO information, illustrations, or quotations relevant to what you've shared.
  • Generate and send your free Coverage Snapshot, where you've requested one.
  • Improve how I explain and present options to you.
  • Keep basic records of client and prospect communications, consistent with standard advisor practice.

I do not use your information for purposes unrelated to your inquiry, and I do not sell your personal information to third parties.

06 Free assessment vs. an actual insurance application

Submitting your information through a Meta lead form, Messenger, a website form, or the free assessment is an inquiry — it lets me understand your situation and get in touch with you. It is not an insurance application, and it does not mean:

  • that you have purchased any insurance or HMO product;
  • that you have been approved, accepted, or enrolled by any insurance company or HMO provider; or
  • that any coverage is currently in effect.

An actual insurance or HMO application is a separate, formal process that happens only if you decide to proceed, and it is subject to the underwriting, approval, and documentation requirements of the relevant insurance company or HMO provider. I do not promise or guarantee approval, coverage terms, or acceptance on behalf of any insurer or HMO provider.

  • Meta Platforms, Inc. (Facebook/Instagram): When you submit a Meta Lead Ad or message my Page, your information is necessarily handled by Meta's platform to deliver it to me, subject to Meta's own Data Policy.

While I take reasonable steps to protect your information, no method of storage or transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and I cannot guarantee absolute security.

I keep your information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy — generally, for the duration of our client or prospect relationship, plus a reasonable period afterward for recordkeeping and any applicable legal, tax, or regulatory requirements. If you ask me to delete your information and there is no legal or contractual reason to keep it, I will do so within a reasonable time.

10 Your rights under the Data Privacy Act

As a data subject under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173), you have the right to:

  • Be informed that your personal information will be, is being, or has been processed.
  • Access your personal information that I hold.
  • Correct any inaccurate or outdated personal information.
  • Object to the processing of your information, including for direct marketing.
  • Erasure or blocking of your personal information under certain conditions.
  • Data portability, where technically feasible.
  • Damages, for any harm from inaccurate, unlawfully obtained, or unauthorized use of your personal information.
  • File a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (NPC) if you believe your rights have been violated.
